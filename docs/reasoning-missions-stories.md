# M3 Daily collection and unknown-start stories

Status: engineering implemented and verified. Parent review of the new Vietnamese
wording and the child pilot remain pending. Nothing has been deployed.

M3 adds two story families to the existing mission loop. They use the same
attempt ledger, offline queue, API, D1 table, parent panel, and Training screen as
`instruction_chain`; there is no second implementation of the loop. No D1
migration is needed because the ledger payload already stores the mission
definition.

## Shared changes

- `ReasoningMission` is the union of the three families. `MissionFamily` is
  `instruction_chain | daily_collection | unknown_start`.
- `validateMission`, `getMissionView`, `getMissionHint`, `getMissionStepFeedback`
  and `getMissionDiagram` dispatch by family (`missions/mission.ts`). The
  instruction-chain generators are unchanged, so saved v1/v2 records still
  validate.
- A step may list `acceptedChoiceIds` for equivalent valid plans. Responses are
  correct when the choice is accepted. `getMissionView` still never returns
  `correctChoiceId`, `acceptedChoiceIds`, or solutions.
- New templates are generated by `daily_collection_v1`, `unknown_start_v1` and
  `unknown_start_v2`. Like the instruction-chain versions, they are frozen once
  released; change content in a new version. Training generates `unknown_start_v2`;
  `unknown_start_v1` stays so saved attempts still validate.
  Parameters are copied field by field and validated against reviewed name, object,
  item, and action lists, so tampered or unknown fields fail regeneration.
- Calculations get their own objective. `calculation` covers answers up to 20 and
  `calculation_over_20` covers larger ones, so the parent can tell a story or plan
  mistake from a mistake adding beyond 20. Independent final answers still
  produce no objective diagnosis.
- `ReasoningSettings.enabledFamilies` accepts any non-duplicated subset of the three
  families. Existing `["instruction_chain"]` settings are still valid. Parents toggle
  each family separately. `ReasoningProgress.families` adds per-family counts and
  the totals remain their sum, so family evidence is not blended.
- Training shows **Chọn loại bài** only when more than one family is enabled. Each
  family's first mission is the parent's original example (15, 13, 48). Missions 2–3
  stay within 20; after that, ranges widen. School and plain wording alternate per
  family. Support (guided/independent) is still chosen by the child or parent.

## Daily collection (`daily_collection`)

Original: Hải has 8 thẻ Kun, gets 1 more each day for 5 days: **13**.
Parameters: `{ name, object, start, perDay, days }`; objects are thẻ Kun, nhãn dán,
viên bi, con tem, and vỏ sò.

| Stage                   | Range                                              |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `easy` (first missions) | 1 per day, 2–6 days, total within 20               |
| `standard`              | 1–5 per day, 2–7 days, start 2–40, total within 99 |

Repeated addition comes first: for 2 per day the second step asks for the whole
added amount, and the worked hint writes `2 + 2 + 2 = 6`. Multiplication notation is
not used.

Guided steps (independent shows only the last):

1. `start_amount` — amount before the collection days.
2. `repeated_change` — total added. Distractors: one day only, a missed day, the
   starting amount, the day count, an extra day.
3. `plan` — `start + added`, vs `start − added`, one day only, missed day, extra day.
4. `final` — total. Distractors cover the same misreadings, including leaving out
   the starting amount.

The school wording is the original sentence. The plain wording reorders the facts
(“Trong 3 ngày, mỗi ngày An nhặt được 2 viên bi. Trước đó An đã có 5 viên bi…”) and
is the held-out transfer form.

## Unknown start (`unknown_start_v1`, loss-only)

v1 only models two **removals**. It is frozen; new Training missions use v2 below.

Original: after eating 4 and giving away 1 chục, Mai has 34 left: **48**.
Parameters: `{ name, item, changes: [two removals], remaining }`. Removals are an
action from the item's reviewed list with a count and a unit (`one` or `chuc`).
The two removals have different actions and different amounts, with at most one
“chục”. Amount ranges are 1–9 or 1–3 chục; the remaining amount is 1–60; the
total is within 99. `easy` has no chục and stays within 20.

Guided steps:

1. `dozen_value` — only if the story has a “chục”: “1 chục cái kẹo là bao nhiêu
   cái kẹo?” Distractors are 1, 12, and 20 (count, dozen read as 12, doubled).
2. `find_unknown` — “Bài toán hỏi số … vào lúc nào?” Choices distinguish lúc đầu from
   the remaining amount and from only one removal.
3. `reverse_plan` — adding both amounts back is correct **in either order**
   (`acceptedChoiceIds`). Distractors: subtract again, or restore one amount only.
4. `final` — distractors: subtract both again, omit either removal, read chục as 1.

Remediation never says “cho means subtract”; it says to give back the amount that was
lost. The plain wording puts the remaining amount first (“Hiện Nam còn 23 viên bi.
Trước đó…”).

## Unknown start v2 (`unknown_start_v2`): gains and losses in any order

Real problems mix “thêm” and “bớt” in any phase, so v2 has two or three events, each a
**gain** or a **loss**, in story order. The original amount is still the unknown.
Parameters: `{ name, item, changes: [{ kind, action, count, unit }, …], remaining }`.

- Loss actions are v1's (ăn hết, cho em gái, cho bạn, dùng, lấy ra, làm mất). Gain actions
  are `được cho thêm`, `mua thêm`, `nhặt thêm`; every gain verb says “thêm” or “được”, so
  the vocabulary never turns on a single word like “cho”.
- Example: after Mai eats 4, is given a chục more, and gives her sister 5, she has 41
  left → **40** (`41 + 5 − 10 + 4`). The original 48 story is also valid v2 (two losses).
- Rules: distinct actions and distinct amounts (after “chục”), at most one “chục”, and the
  amount must stay between 1 and 99 at every point of the story.
- Generation: two events (one gain, one loss, either first) in `easy`, all within 20,
  no “chục”; two or three events in `standard` (one kind may repeat), 65% with a “chục”,
  within 99. Both kinds always appear in generated missions.

Steps are the same four as v1 (`dozen_value` only with a chục). Differences:

- `find_unknown` choices: lúc đầu, sau tất cả các việc, chỉ sau việc đầu, chỉ sau việc cuối.
- `reverse_plan`: each event has one operation — undo a loss with “thêm lại”, undo a gain
  with “bớt đi”. Undoing every event is correct in either story or rewind order
  (`acceptedChoiceIds`). Distractors always include repeating each event's direction
  (“Bớt tiếp … thêm …”), plus one of: undoing only the losses, undoing only the gains, or
  restoring one event only.
- `final` distractors: same direction, add or subtract everything, ignore the gains,
  ignore the losses, and chục read as 1.
- Hints and diagrams show both operations, e.g. `41 → + 5 → − 10 → + 4 → Lúc đầu: ?`.
  Remediation says to undo what each event did, never that a word means a sign.

## Rewind and day-slot visuals

Guided missions show `getMissionDiagram` under the problem. Unanswered steps stay
blank so the picture never gives the answer away:

- Daily collection: `Có sẵn: □ → Ngày 1: □ … → Tất cả: ?`; each part fills in after
  its step is answered correctly.
- Unknown start: `Lúc đầu: ? → ăn hết 4 → cho em gái 1 chục → Còn lại: 34`. “1 chục = 10”
  appears after the dozen step. The backward row `34 → +10 → +4 → Lúc đầu: ?` appears
  only after the plan step is answered.

Independent practice has no diagram, intermediate prompts, or solution.

## Arithmetic above 20

M3 does not add a separate warm-up quiz. Instead, easy missions stay within 20 and
later ones go up to 99, and the final step's objective is `calculation_over_20`
when the answer is above 20. The parent panel reports it separately from
`calculation` and from the story objectives. If a child struggles only there, the
fix is arithmetic practice, not more story practice. A standalone prerequisite quiz
can be added if the pilot shows this evidence is insufficient.

## Verification

All 672 tests pass: 293 engine, 62 API, 317 web. They cover independent simulations of
the answer for 1,000 seeds × 2 stages per family, bounded values, unique choices,
deterministic replay, tampering rejection, the original and held-out examples, both
accepted add-back orders, per-family progress, multi-family settings, the Training
flow for each family, diagrams staying blank, and the API replaying and rejecting
forged ledgers. Type checks, lint, and the production build pass.

**Deploy order:** deploy the API before clients. An older API rejects the new
families in settings and the new mission templates; an older client treats settings
that include a new family as unsupported.
